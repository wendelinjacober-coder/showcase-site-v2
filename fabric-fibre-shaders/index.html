How it works

Smart Controls

The handful of sliders that matter for this exact shader, plus Variation: a soft, large-scale colour and gloss shift that breaks the clean CG look of a perfect surface.

Surface layers and wear

Edge Wear, Cavity Dirt, Dust, Wet, Rust, Moss, Scratches and Fingerprints, each tuned to the material: bare metal in its own colour, see-through smudges on glass, whitened scuffs on plastic. One click presets: Clean, Used, Old, Weathered.

Paint where it goes

Paint any layer by hand in Vertex Paint: white adds it, black removes it. The procedural detail stays inside your strokes.

Decals

Project logos, labels and stickers onto any object with a movable image empty. Opacity, gloss and metal per decal; dirt and dust lie on top.

Real-world scale

A brick is a brick on a cup and on a wall. Switch to fit-to-object at any time. Mapping: Object, Sticky (glued to animated meshes) or UV (follows your unwrap).

Real displacement

Real geometry where the relief is deep enough to see, matched to each shader's own bump, with adaptive subdivision and Micro Detail. Shallow relief stays fast bump.

Asset Browser

Every shader also sits in Blender's Asset Browser, sorted by category, ready to drag and drop. No UVs, no image textures.

Geometry Nodes tools

Real geometry on your object: Faux Fur, Short Animal Fur, Teddy Plush, Peach Fuzz, Felt Surface, Pompom and more.
